Market Drivers

Why Florida Roofing Outperforms

Multiple structural tailwinds create consistent, durable demand regardless of season.

🌀

Hurricane Season

Central FL saw an 18% surge in roof claims in early 2026. Each major storm event generates $500M–$2B in roofing work statewide.

Impact Level: Very High
📋

Insurance Requirements

Insurers now require roof replacement on homes 15–20 years old, even without visible damage. Florida's 25% replacement rule drives full re-roofs.

Impact Level: High
🏘️

Aging Housing Stock

Millions of Florida roofs built in the 1990s–2000s boom are now hitting replacement age. Lifespan of asphalt shingles: 20–25 years.

Impact Level: High
🌱

State Grant Programs

My Safe Florida Home grants up to $10,000 per household for hurricane mitigation improvements — direct roofing stimulus.

Impact Level: Medium
📈

Population Growth

Florida remains a top destination for domestic migration. New residential construction + re-roofing demand = dual revenue streams.

Impact Level: High
⚖️

Tort Reform Tailwind

2023 landmark tort reform eliminated AOB abuse. Insurance carriers re-entering Florida, softening market, 5–10% rate cuts projected in 2026.

Impact Level: Medium

Option 1 · Build from Scratch

Getting Licensed & Operational in Florida

Florida has some of the strictest roofing contractor licensing in the US — but it creates a defensible market.

📋 Licensing Requirements

  • Pass Florida DBPR Roofing Contractor Exam (2 parts)
  • 4 years verifiable roofing experience minimum
  • FICO score ≥ 660 (or financial stability bond)
  • General liability insurance (required by law)
  • Workers' compensation coverage
  • Certified license = statewide operating privileges
License fee: $145–$245 per biennial cycle

Decision Framework

Which Path Is Right for You?

The optimal choice depends on your available capital, risk tolerance, timeline, and existing roofing industry experience.

🏗️

Choose Build if…

  • You have 4+ years of roofing experience
  • You can deploy $60–80K comfortably
  • You want zero debt obligations
  • You have a specific niche or market angle
  • You're comfortable with an 18–24 month ramp period
  • You want complete control over culture & systems
Best for:
Experienced roofers becoming operators, or capital-constrained builders willing to play a longer game.
🤝

Choose Acquire if…

  • You can deploy $100–350K for down + fees
  • You want cash flow from Day 1
  • You're comfortable managing a team you didn't build
  • You're business-operations oriented vs. trade-skilled
  • You want to accelerate to scale faster
  • You can qualify for a FICO 650+ SBA loan
Best for:
Business buyers, entrepreneurs with capital, or investors looking for a high-cash-flow asset in a durable sector.
🔑

Hybrid strategy: Some operators use Option 1 (scratch build) to learn the market for 12–18 months at low risk, then use early profits + SBA to acquire a competitor and immediately 3-5x their scale. This is the path PE firms have used to consolidate the industry.
